Operating checklist

  • Apply to relevant affiliate programs using accurate business and traffic information.
  • Wait for approval and use only links supplied by the program.
  • Check product price, stock, shipping, and seller legitimacy before publishing.
  • Disclose affiliate relationships clearly beside recommendations and links.
  • Do not claim a deal is the lowest price unless you have evidence.
  • Review commission reversals, payout minimums, payment timing, and Alaska eligibility.
